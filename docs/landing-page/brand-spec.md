The mobile identity leads the public brand. The web application's warm, calm operational hierarchy remains a supporting influence, especially for product screenshots and dense proof sections.

## Brand invariants

- Preserve the interlocking navy and emerald shield and arrow geometry.
- Use the flat symbol in the navigation, footer, favicon and compact UI.
- Reserve the dimensional symbol for the hero or one authored brand moment.
- Keep the public interface light first, with deep navy structure and emerald action.
- Use amber and red only to communicate a real warning, deadline or risk.
- Never redraw the symbol with CSS or substitute a generic shield icon.
- Never fabricate customer logos, testimonials, compliance percentages or savings claims.

## Asset treatment

- Keep brand and 3D safety assets on transparent backgrounds.
- Use the existing soft studio lighting and matte material language for any added 3D icons.
- Do not mix photorealistic PPE, flat line art and the current soft 3D set inside the same visual cluster.
- Product imagery must use real NexaSST screens. Generated visuals may extend the safety object world, but must not invent a product interface.

## Typography

- Marketing UI: Manrope, one family throughout the page.
- Product screenshots: preserve the platform and system typography already present in the apps.
- Wordmark: use the existing brand lockup where available; otherwise pair the official symbol with the `NexaSST` name as a navigation label, not as a replacement asset.
- No italics and no 900 weight in landing copy. Use weight 700 at most.

## Recommended additions to the 3D icon family

1. Fire extinguisher with gauge, for inspection evidence and asset readiness.
2. Clipboard with check and attached photo, for traceable inspections.
3. Training certificate with clock, for validity and retraining deadlines.
4. Warning triangle with magnifier, for early risk identification.
5. Smartphone scanning a QR code, for field flow and asset traceability.
6. Protective glove, for PPE completeness without repeating the helmet.
7. Safety boot, for a broader PPE set.
8. Industrial machine guard or lockout padlock, for pre task controls.
9. Ergonomic chair or human posture model, for AEP and AET.
10. Folder with shield and timeline, for evidence, audit trail and defensibility.

Highest priority for the landing page: extinguisher, clipboard evidence, certificate with clock, QR phone, and risk magnifier.
